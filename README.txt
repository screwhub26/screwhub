# Screw Hub Website

This is a static website made for Screw Hub. It can be hosted without Wix Business Premium.

## 1. Add your contact details
Open `script.js` and replace:
- YOUR_9999951859
- YOUR_WHATSAPP_NUMBER_WITH_COUNTRY_CODE
- YOUR_EMAIL@example.com

For India, WhatsApp should be in international format, for example `919999951859` (without + or spaces).

## 2. Preview
Double-click `index.html` on your computer.

## 3. Publish
Upload the entire folder to a static host such as Cloudflare Pages, GitHub Pages, Netlify or another static hosting provider.

## 4. Connect the Wix-purchased domain
After the hosting service gives you its custom-domain/DNS instructions, add the required DNS records in the Wix domain/DNS management area. The exact records depend on the hosting provider.

## Website sections
Home, Products, About Us, Why Us, Contact / Quick Enquiry.
